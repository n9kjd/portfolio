My name is Jeffrey Dreyer. I am a senior photojournalism student at Ball State University. I am an active member of the National Press Photographers Association, including serving as an executive officer, and a regular attendee to the Atlanta Photojournalism Seminar.

My experience includes service as a Multimedia Editor at the Ball State Daily News, freelancing with Free Press Indiana and Mirror Indy, and travelling to Washington, D.C., Mexico, and France.
